DDM645 Digital Multimeter w/ 20,000 Count Display

The DDM645 multimeter is a professional hand-held meter capable of measuring AC/DC voltage, AC/DC current, resistance, capacitance, frequency, continuity, diodes, and hFe transistor gain. This meter is ideal for professionals and hobbyists alike.

Features:

Measurement
  • DC Voltage: 1000V
  • AC Voltage: 750V
  • DC Current: 200mA, 10A
  • AC Current: 200mA, 10A
  • Resistance: 20MΩ
  • Capacitance: 100µF
  • Frequency: 20kHz
  • Continuity: <50Ω, buzzer sounds
  • hFE Transistor Gain: 0-1000
Aditional Features:

  • LCD Display: 20,000 Counts
  • Auto Power Off
  • Data Hold
  • Low Battery Indicator
  • Safety Rating: CAT. II 1000V, CAT. III 600V
Accessories:
  • Test Leads
  • 1 x 9V Battery
  • Multifunction adapter
  • User's Manual

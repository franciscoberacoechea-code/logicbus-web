DDM644 digital multimeter with range selection by rotary switch

The DDM644 is a professional handheld digital multimeter. This manual range meter is easy to use and is ideal for professionals and amateurs alike.


Features:

Measurement
  • AC/DC Voltage
  • AC/DC Current
  • Resistance
  • Capacitance
  • Frequency
  • Continuity
  • Diodes
  • hFE transistor gain
  • Temperature (°C)
Aditional Features:

  • 2000 count LCD display
  • Auto power off
  • Data hold
  • Low battery indicator
  • Safety Rating: CAT. II 1000V, CAT. III 600V
Accessories:
  • Test Leads
  • Multifunction adapter
  • Type-K thermocouple
  • 1x 9V battery
  • User's Manual


  • DC Voltage: 1000V
  • AC Voltage: 750V
  • DC Current: 200mA, 10A
  • AC Current: 200mA, 10A
  • Resistance: 200MΩ
  • Capacitance: 100µF
  • Frequency: 20MHz
  • Continuity: <50Ω, buzzer sounds
  • hFE Transistor Gain: 0-1000
  • Temperature: -20~1000°C

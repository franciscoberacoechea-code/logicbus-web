DDM190D Pen-Type Digital Multimeter

The DDM190D is a portable pen-type digital multimeter featuring 5V logic level test, DC/AC voltage and current measurement, auto/manual range, maximum and hold reading, auto power off, and LCD built in for easy reading. The slim and lightweight design with retractable probe is mobile and ideal for both professionals and hobbyists.

DDM450 TRMS compact digital multimeter

The DDM450 is a small, handheld, true-RMS digital multimeter. It's small design makes it easy to use with one hand while still being a professional grade meter. With TRMS capabilities, this meter provides the high level of accuracy normally found in a larger meter.


Features:

Measurement
  • AC/DC Voltage
  • AC/DC Current
  • Resistance
  • Continuity
  • Diodes
  • 1.5V and 9V battery test
Aditional Features:

  • 4000 count LCD display
  • Auto-ranging
  • True RMS
  • Non-contact voltage detector
  • Auto power off
  • Data hold
  • Display backlight
  • Low battery indicator
  • Safety Rating: CAT. III 600V
Accessories:
  • Test Leads
  • 9V Battery
  • User's Manual
